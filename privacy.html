Privacy Notice

Pranav Distributors & Logistics (PDL) respects the privacy of people who contact us through this website. This notice describes how information submitted through our enquiry, career and transport partner forms is intended to be handled.

Information we collect

Depending on the form, we may collect your name, contact details, company information, service requirements, collection and delivery locations, career information, vehicle details and documents you choose to upload.

How we use information

We use submitted information to respond to enquiries, assess service requirements, review career applications, evaluate potential transport partnerships and maintain related business records.

Access and protection

Website submissions are stored in the PDL administration system. Access is intended to be limited to authorised personnel. Uploaded documents are stored outside the public website directory and require an authenticated administrator session to retrieve.

Retention and requests

PDL should retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected or as required by applicable obligations. To request access, correction or deletion, contact info@pdl-logistics.com.sg.
